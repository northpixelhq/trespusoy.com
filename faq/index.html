The game

What is Tres – Pusoy Dos?

Pusoy Dos — the Filipino card classic, also known as Big Two — played solo against three AI opponents, with friends on the same Wi-Fi, or online with friends in a private room. No sign-in needed.

It is made by North Pixel Labs.

Is it free?

Yes. Tres is free and shows ads: full-screen ads between games, and one small banner on Home. Never in the middle of a hand, and never in Online rooms. Watching an ad to earn Cosmetic Tokens is always your choice, and nothing in the game is locked behind an ad. Offline, there are no ads at all.

There is an optional Shop. Every purchase is cosmetic only: it changes how the table looks and sounds, never the cards, rules, or opponent strength.

Which phones can play it?

Android phones, from Google Play, and iPhone and iPad, from the App Store.

Which rules does it play?

Pusoy Dos · Philippines by default: diamonds high, no A2345 or 23456 straights, and the 3 of clubs opens every game. It also offers four more rule sets: Big 2 · Malaysia, global, Capsa Banting · Indonesia, 大老二 · Da Lao Er · Taiwan and 鋤大D · Chor Dai Di · Hong Kong. How to play teaches the rules card by card.

How strong are the AI opponents?

You choose Easy, Standard or Expert.

Easy: the AI plays its weakest cards first and doesn’t keep track of what’s been played. It won’t break up a five-card hand to beat a play, and about one time in four it passes on a play it could have beaten.

Standard: the AI keeps track of every card played and how many cards each player has left. It saves its strongest cards for later and plays to stop anyone down to two cards from going out.

Expert: the AI keeps track like Standard, then looks ahead: it plays out its best options against guesses at the other hands, made from what’s been played and who passed.

At every level, it never sees your cards.

Can I learn the game in the app?

Yes. A short, skippable tutorial covers the basics, and the full rules are one tap away whenever you need them. Switch on “Highlight playable cards” while you are learning, and switch it off when you would rather work it out yourself.

Offline and with friends

Can I play without the internet?

Yes. Solo and same Wi-Fi play run on your devices. Start a hand on the bus, in a queue, or with no signal at all: only Online play needs the internet.

How do I play with friends in the same room?

Put every phone on the same Wi-Fi: up to 4 players, no internet and no account. One person hosts a room, and friends join with its Room Code or the host’s QR code. AI fills any open seats. See the steps.

How do I play with friends who are far away?

Make a private Online room and share its code, or invite a friend by their Player Code. Everyone at the table is invited or approved, AI fills any open seats, and there are no ads in Online rooms. See the steps.

Can Android and iPhone players play together?

Yes, in Online rooms: Android, iPhone and iPad players play together there. For a group with both, make an Online room.

Is there public matchmaking or chat?

No public matchmaking and no typed chat — just quick expressions to tap. Everyone at an Online table is invited or approved.

Do I need an account?

No. Solo and same Wi-Fi play need no account, and Online play needs no sign-in: it makes a private guest profile for you.

Progress, purchases and privacy

What if I close the app in the middle of a game?

Close the app in the middle of a hand and come back to the same table — same cards, same turn, same position.

Can I keep my progress on a new phone?

On Android, if you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, your Milestones appear as achievements on your Play Games profile, and your progress can be backed up to your own Google account and restored on another phone.

On iPhone and iPad, if you sign in to Game Center, your progress can be backed up to your own iCloud and restored on another iPhone or iPad signed in to Game Center with the same Apple Account.

The two backups are separate: moving between Android and an iPhone or iPad carries neither. A purchase belongs to the store it was made in: Google Play purchases are restored on Android, and App Store purchases on iPhone and iPad.

The game is complete without either sign-in. Your Online profile is separate: on Android you can protect it with Play Games, and on iPhone and iPad it stays on that device.

What can I buy, and what does it change?

Unlock complete table themes with Cosmetic Tokens earned by playing and collecting Milestones, or buy a theme or token bundle in the optional Shop. Every purchase is cosmetic only: it changes how the table looks and sounds, never the cards, rules, or opponent strength.

Google Play on Android and the App Store on iPhone and iPad handle Shop payments; North Pixel Labs does not receive your payment-card details.

What does Tres collect?

Local play runs on your device. Nearby play sends room information to the other phones on your local network. Optional Online play uses our server and a private guest profile; no sign-in is required.

Google serves the ads, outside Online rooms. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect data from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device information, under Google's own privacy policy. You choose whether ads may be personalized; if you decline, you see non-personalized ads.

There are no North Pixel Labs gameplay analytics, and North Pixel Labs never sells player data. The privacy policy says exactly what is stored and sent, and for how long.

Who is Tres for?

The game is intended for players 13 and older and is not directed at children.

How do I delete my Online profile?

Use Online profile → Delete online profile in the app and confirm, or email northpixellabs@gmail.com to request help or deletion from outside the app.

On iPhone and iPad, delete your Online profile in the app before you delete the app or change devices if you want it removed sooner. Uninstalling does not itself delete it. Delete your Online profile has the details.

Does it work with a screen reader?

Switch on four-colour suits for faster reading, and size the text to suit you.

On Android, every play at the table is narrated by your screen reader. On iPhone and iPad, narration with VoiceOver has not been confirmed yet, so we don’t promise it there.
