The rules you grew up with

Beat what is on the table, or pass.

The 3 of clubs opens every game. Play a single, a pair, a trio or a five-card hand — straight, flush, full house, four-of-a-kind, straight flush — then beat what is on the table, or pass. When everyone passes, the trick is yours to lead.

Shown here: the rules of Pusoy Dos · Philippines, the game's default.

Real play, recorded from Tres 2.5.0, solo against three AI opponents. The waits before each play are cut short.

  1. A new game deals thirteen cards each.
  2. You open with a club flush. The first play must include the 3 of clubs, and this one does.
  3. An AI opponent beats it with a hearts flush. Between two flushes the suit decides first, so hearts beat clubs — even clubs topped by a 2.
  4. Another game: you have already finished 1st. An AI opponent goes out 3rd with a full house, the game ends, and your win lights up the table.
  5. You finished first. You win!

Five-card hands, lowest to highest

  1. 78910JStraightFive ranks in a row. A 2 never plays in one.
  2. 3710K2FlushFive cards of one suit.
  3. 5551010Full HouseA trio and a pair.
  4. 99994Four-of-a-KindFour of one rank, plus any one card.
  5. 678910Straight FlushA straight, all in one suit.

A higher hand beats a lower one, whatever its cards.

The moment a player runs out of cards the trick ends right there, and the next player leads a fresh one. Every game runs all the way out to a full 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th place — it is not over the moment someone wins.

Know the order

From 3 up to 2.

The 3 is the lowest rank and the 2 the highest. When two cards share a rank, the suit decides: clubs, then spades, then hearts, with diamonds on top. So the 3 of clubs is the lowest card in the deck — the one that opens the game — and the 2 of diamonds is the highest.

These are the rules of Pusoy Dos · Philippines, the game's default. It also offers four more rule sets: Big 2 · Malaysia, global, Capsa Banting · Indonesia, 大老二 · Da Lao Er · Taiwan and 鋤大D · Chor Dai Di · Hong Kong.

Ads, sign-in and privacy

Honest about ads

Tres is free and shows ads: full-screen ads between games, and one small banner on Home. Never in the middle of a hand, and never in Online rooms. Watching an ad to earn tokens is always your choice, and nothing in the game is locked behind one. Offline, there are no ads at all.

No sign-in needed

Solo and same Wi-Fi play need no account. Online play needs no sign-in either: it makes a private guest profile for you.

Clear about privacy

Solo and same Wi-Fi play run on your devices and do not contact our Online service. Google serves the ads and may collect data such as your device's advertising ID to do it; you choose whether ads may be personalized. The privacy policy says exactly what is stored and sent.
