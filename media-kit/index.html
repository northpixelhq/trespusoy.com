At a glance

Game
Pusoy Dos, the Filipino card classic, also known as Big Two
Platforms
Android, on Google Play; iPhone and iPad, on the App Store
Price
Free, with ads (full-screen ads between games and a small banner on Home; never in the middle of a hand or in Online rooms) and optional in-app purchases

The names

  • Tres – Pusoy Dos is the game’s name: Tres, an en dash (–) with a space on each side, then Pusoy Dos.
  • Tres on its own is the short form, and the name the game shows on its own screens.
  • North Pixel Labs makes the game.
  • Pusoy Dos is the card game it plays, also known as Big Two, Filipino Poker, or simply Dos.
