Play online with friends

Make a private room and share its code, or invite a friend by their Player Code. Everyone at the table is invited or approved, AI fills any open seats, and there are no ads in Online rooms.

Online, in a private room

Online play needs the internet, and no sign-in. Android, iPhone and iPad players play together.

  1. On Home, tap Play together, then Online.

    The first time, choose your name at the table and agree to the Terms. There is no sign-in.

  2. Tap Create room.

    You get a room code to share with your friends. Copy it, or send it with Share text.

  3. A friend taps Join a room, types the code and taps Request to join.

    You let them in with Let in.

  4. Or invite a friend by their Player Code.

    Invite by player code sends an invitation. Invitations don’t send a notification: ask your friend to open Online in Tres to accept it. Recent players lets you invite someone you played with again in one tap.

  5. Friends tap I’m ready, and you start the game.

    AI fills any open seats.

  6. After a game, friends tap Ready for rematch and you tap Rematch.

    One code all evening: the next game deals at once.

At an Online table

  • No public matchmaking and no typed chat — just quick expressions to tap.
  • The Host can turn on an optional turn timer, and every Online Result shows its Game Time.
  • If a friend leaves mid-game, the AI plays their seat and the game goes on. If the Host ends the game, it ends for everyone, with no Result.
  • Online rooms play Pusoy Dos · Philippines or 鋤大D · Chor Dai Di · Hong Kong.
  • There are no ads in Online rooms. Online games do not earn Cosmetic Tokens or Milestones.

On the same Wi-Fi

Same Wi-Fi, up to 4 players: no internet and no account. AI fills any open seats. Playing with both Android phones and iPhones? Use an Online room.

  1. Put every phone on the same Wi-Fi.

    Everyone needs the same version of Tres, so update together.

  2. One person taps Play together, then Nearby, then Host a room.

  3. Friends tap Join a room.

    They pick the room on this Wi-Fi and type its Room Code — or scan the host’s QR code.

  4. The host starts the game.

    AI fills any open seats, and the host picks the room’s rules for everyone.

Can’t see the room?

Check that both phones are on the same Wi-Fi, and that the host has already tapped Host a room.

Scanning the host’s QR code works even when the room won’t appear in the list.

Which to choose

Same room, same Wi-Fi

Everyone on one Wi-Fi network: up to 4 players, no internet and no account.

Far apart, or Android with iPhone

Play Online. Android, iPhone and iPad players play together in Online rooms.

Your Online profile

Online play makes a private guest profile for you; no sign-in is required. On Android you can protect it with Play Games, to keep it if you reinstall or change phones. On iPhone and iPad it stays on that device.

To delete it, use Online profile → Delete online profile in the app, or email northpixellabs@gmail.com. On iPhone and iPad, delete your Online profile in the app before you delete the app or change devices if you want it removed sooner. Read the Online Terms and Delete your Online profile for the details.
